CSSE/MA384 - Data Mining

Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology

Computer Science and Software Engineering and Mathematics Departments

Winter 2026-2027

Description

An introduction to data mining for large data sets, including data preparation, exploration, aggregation/reduction, and visualization. Elementary methods for classification, association, and cluster analysis are covered. Significant attention will be given to presenting and reporting data mining results. CSSE384 and MA384 are the same course.

Prerequisites

  • Credit Hours: 4R-0L-4C
  • Prerequisites: CSSE 120 and MA 221 and (MA 223 or MA 381). Future offerings will instead require CHE310 or CSSE220 or MA332 or MA386 or ME227.
  • Corequisites: None
  • Skill with Python is helpful. If you don't know some, be ready to dive in!
